Datasets
--------
No new dataset was acquired. Searched this phase: Zenodo (evapotranspiration products), Mendeley
Data (multi-sector precision-irrigation telemetry), Figshare (LED-spectrum supplements), NASA OSDR
(OSD-480 Plant Gravity Perception, OSD-411, GLDS-251), journal supplements (Plants 12:463,
Plant Soil Environ 70:305, Agronomy). Every result was rejected with a reason recorded in
docs/PHASE35_DATA_ACQUISITION.md. The short version: they are either remote-sensing or
reanalysis products, papers or registrations, open-field systems that cannot validate a controlled-
environment water balance, or transcriptomic experiments without measured growth outcomes.
