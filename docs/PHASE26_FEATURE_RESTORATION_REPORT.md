# Phase 26 — Feature Restoration Report

Twelve named capabilities were audited against a five-tier readiness framework, and two of them
gained evidence. This report is about the two, and about the five features that were deliberately
left alone.

Generated artefact: `docs/PHASE26_FEATURE_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`, built from
`data/research/phase26/feature_readiness` by `ml/research/build_feature_readiness.py`.

## 1. The readiness scale

A tier describes what a feature may **claim**, not how much code it has.

| Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `production-ready` | independent evaluation exists and the stated limits match what it found |
| `research-ready` | measured data and a committed evaluation exist; the claimed level is not identifiable from them |
| `experimental` | implemented and internally evaluated; no independent validation exists |
| `data-blocked` | no obtainable dataset can move the feature; the block is the data's absence |
| `unsupported` | the product must not make the claim at all |

The builder refuses to write an audit whose tiers outrun its evidence, and the test suite includes a
test that feeds it a fabricated `production-ready` feature and asserts rejection. A feature may not
be `production-ready` without a dataset, may not be `research-ready` without artefacts that exist on
disk, and may not be `data-blocked` while naming an acquired dataset.

## 3. Feature 1 — measured crop water use (the substantive restoration)

### What was built

An out-of-sample comparison between the platform's unmodified water path and a third-party
measurement: `ml/evaluation/phase26_measured_et_validation`, generated by
`ml/research/validate_measured_et.py`.

Measured side: 35 published pots of greenhouse lettuce, 11 weighings, **380 usable pot-days**, per-pot
gravimetric evapotranspiration. Simulated side: `water.daily_water_demand_l` driven by
`growth.logistic_progress(day, 35)` with the lettuce crop profile — the shipped code, no constant
altered.

### What it found

**Shape.** Spearman **0.7909** between treatment-mean measured ET and simulated demand across the 13
measurement days (Pearson 0.5631). The seasonal shape is broadly right.

**Level.** Not identifiable, and the artefact demonstrates this rather than asserting it:

| Denominator | Measured | Platform's 2.5 L m⁻² d⁻¹ flux | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pot surface (0.0112 m², assumption) | 10.108 L m⁻² d⁻¹ | 0.24 × measured | platform is ~4× **low** |
| canopy leaf area (0.13 m², cross-experiment) | 0.871 L m⁻² d⁻¹ | 2.75 × measured | platform is ~2.871× **high** |

The sign of the discrepancy reverses with a denominator the study does not publish. The spread
between those two readings is wider than either discrepancy. **No constant was changed, no crop
coefficient was published, and `peak_water_l_per_day` is untouched** — a test asserts
`simulatedSide.constantsChanged == []`.

A single fitted scale of 6.38 is reported *next to* the uncalibrated numbers, never instead of them,
with a leave-one-treatment-out range of 5.88–6.61. Its interpretation is spelled out: it absorbs the
pot-area assumption, the untranspiring substrate surface and the evaporation component, so it is not
evidence that peak water use is 6.38× the profile value.

**Per-gram fresh mass**, the one denominator the data actually supports: measured 0.6054 mL g⁻¹ d⁻¹
against simulated 0.0848, a ratio of 0.1416. This removes the unpublished geometry but not the
evaporation component.

### What this does and does not close

The gap `measured-eta` moves from `no-data` to `partially-addressed` with **severity unchanged at
blocking**. It closes exactly one clause — that no measured crop water use of any kind existed in
the repository. Four blockers survive and are named in the gap matrix:

1. evapotranspiration includes substrate evaporation; transpiration is not separated;
2. no per-day trial weather, so no reference ET0 and no crop coefficient can be derived;
3. one cultivar, one greenhouse, four weeks, pot culture;
4. the trial is terrestrial — no reduced-gravity information.

### How this feature must now be described

> Water demand follows a crop-relative growth curve. Its seasonal shape is consistent with an
> independent greenhouse lettuce trial (rank correlation 0.7909 across 11 weighings). Its absolute
> level is **not validated**: the one measured dataset available does not publish the geometry
> needed to convert per-pot water loss into a flux, and the sign of the discrepancy depends on that
> missing geometry.

That is a smaller claim than "water use is validated" and a much larger one than "no measured crop
water use exists". Both halves matter.

## 4. Feature 11 — nutrient solution management

Moved `data-blocked` → `experimental` on the strength of one verified, licensed acquisition
(Halbert-Howard 2020, CC BY 4.0) that reports shoot and fruit nutrient uptake, solution
concentrations and a recycled-solution fraction. It is **acquired but not yet parsed**, so the tier
says `experimental` and not more: no nutrient model consumes it, replication is at row level rather
than per plant, and the workbook reports uptake rather than depletion-and-replenishment. The
product makes no nutrient claim today.

## 5. The features deliberately not touched

Five features had no evidence path forward and stayed where they were. Naming them is the point:

- **fractional gravity** (`data-blocked`) — no open dataset, at any price found in this phase. The
  response curve's sensitivity coefficient remains a documented assumption.
- **root-zone water status** (`data-blocked`) — the Schröder workbook's water-holding-capacity sheet
  is a static substrate property, not a root-zone time series. Capacity is not status.
- **imagery** (`data-blocked`) — the tomato trial ships an RGB-derived leaf-area series, which is a
  measurement *derived from* imagery, not imagery. It cannot support a vision claim.
- **long-run climate** (`data-blocked`) — the downscaled CMIP6 product is catalogued at class D and
  was not acquired; the ET0 target is the observed recent past by construction.
- **LED spectrum response** (`experimental`) and **cultivar metadata** (`experimental`) — both gained
  adjacent datasets in this phase (a spectrum trial with PPFD held constant; three single-cultivar
  experiments) and neither can support more than it already claims. The spectrum dataset holds PPFD
  and photoperiod constant by design, so it cannot separate a spectral effect from a total-light
  effect, and no in-repo experiment carries a cultivar label usable as a model input.

## 6. UI/API compatibility

No API contract, route, category name, service state or UI element was changed. The restoration is
artefact-level: new evaluation JSON, new processed tables, new generated documentation, new tests.
`backend/app/science_classification.py` is untouched, so all 18 model endpoints keep their category,
evidence text and service state.
