Note on the visual-regression baselines
---------------------------------------
docs/screenshots/mission-*.png and the radiation baselines are older than the current layout and
`scripts/visual_regression.py` reports canvas-size mismatches against them. Those mismatches are
pre-existing: a build of the parent commit `bcd4ec5…` reproduces the same current layout, and a
direct parent-vs-branch capture at 1440 px gives an identical 1440x1649 canvas with 0.0351 % of
pixels differing. The baselines were deliberately NOT refreshed, because doing so would have
recorded unrelated layout drift under this phase; the honest measurement is the parent-build one.
ml/evaluation/phase9_visual_regression.json was left as committed rather than overwritten with a
failing run that says nothing about Phase 33. Flagged for the next phase to handle deliberately.
