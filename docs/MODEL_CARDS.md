## `lettuce-earth-growth-v1`

*Earth-only fresh shoot mass regression*

**Purpose.** Predict fresh shoot mass per lettuce plant from thermal time, air and root-zone temperature and incident PPFD, for a controlled-environment lettuce crop.

**Intended use.** Exploring how a change in temperature or light moves per-plant fresh mass inside a plant-factory envelope, and producing a number a researcher can compare against their own measurement.

**Not intended for**

- Operational or mission planning: the model fails its own independent check (41.6 % MAPE against a 30 % criterion fixed before the measurement).
- Crops, cultivars or light regimes outside the declared domain; the model refuses rather than extrapolating.
- Any claim about spaceflight performance. The training data are terrestrial.

**Training data.** committed-file — `0b6072cc4ff65ef6bcc9fd33481d96c76b710945b81e4452a78dd605349feb43`

  SHA-256 of data/processed/lettuce_earth.csv exactly as committed, which is the table train_lettuce.py writes and every lettuce trainer reads. Covers 1648 plant rows x 7 columns; the file is deterministic because the transform from the WUR workbook is fixed in the training script.

**Code.** `ml/training/train_lettuce.py` at `4982b84fbb2c880935f92ae2a5283e81d84e1764` (present in commit: yes; unchanged since: yes)

**Split.** Nested leave-one-experiment-out cross-validation (4 outer, 3 inner folds). Grouped by experiment (4 groups).

**Uncertainty.** 90 % cross-conformal (CV+) interval, radius 112.94 g/plant, measured held-out coverage 0.939 over 4 calibration groups. The artifact records its own caveat: the interval describes this dataset's spread and is not a guarantee for a new greenhouse.

**Evidence.** `fails-independent-check`, decision-grade: no. Not promoted, retrained or relabelled by this metadata completion.

**Completeness.** 15/15 checks.

**Limitations**

- Not NASA or spaceflight data.
- Tip-burn-related treatment omissions create selection bias.
- Plant identifiers are reused across experiments; split by whole experiment.
- Only Batavia Othilie lettuce on Earth; not calibrated for partial gravity or radiation.
- No inference for water stress, nutrient stress, CO2 response, other cultivars, or different photoperiods.
- Measured domain is 2–29 days after transplant, air/root 20–32 C, PPFD 200–750 µmol/m²/s; treatment grid is sparse.
- Environmental combinations omitted for tip burn are unsupported even inside marginal ranges.
- Fresh shoot mass per plant is not edible harvest per square meter; no planting-density conversion is assumed.

## `lettuce-earth-growth-v3`

*Earth fresh shoot mass per plant, mediated by fresh yield per square metre*

**Purpose.** Predict lettuce fresh mass per plant and per square metre from thermal time, temperature and daily light integral, with plant density entering through an explicit per-area identity.

**Intended use.** Bounding how light integral and density move yield per square metre, and comparing a vertical-farm configuration with a horizontal one at equal photon supply.

**Not intended for**

- Operational forecasting: 41.6 % MAPE on the independent Touliatos study against a 30 % criterion fixed in advance. The artifact records this and the platform must not present it as validated.
- Densities above the declared ceiling (~200 plants/m2), where per-area yield is not learned but only divided.
- Any cultivar other than Batavia Othilie: cultivar is not a feature.

**Training data.** committed-file — `0b6072cc4ff65ef6bcc9fd33481d96c76b710945b81e4452a78dd605349feb43`

  SHA-256 of data/processed/lettuce_earth.csv. This value is corroborated rather than merely asserted: the v3 artifact records the same digest in its own trainingDataSha256 field, written by the training run.

**Code.** `ml/training/train_lettuce_v3.py` at `941174fba2d61a9953e0a2dc61608b6d4e1f6e9f` (present in commit: yes; unchanged since: yes)

**Split.** Leave-one-experiment-out, 4 folds. Grouped by experiment (4 groups).

**Uncertainty.** Conformal residual quantiles on the log target, 5th and 95th percentile. Out-of-fold coverage 0.899 on the same predictions the quantiles come from, which the artifact itself labels descriptive rather than independent evidence; the independent cross-campaign check covers 6 of 6 treatment means.

**Evidence.** `fails-independent-check`, decision-grade: no. Not promoted, retrained or relabelled by this metadata completion.

**Completeness.** 15/15 checks.

**Limitations**

- Fresh yield per square metre is higher in the training system (non-limiting CO2, optimal temperature, plant factory) than in an ambient-CO2 greenhouse, so an upward bias remains.
- Density enters only through the per-area identity; the model does not learn how density changes per-area yield, and refuses above ~200 plants/m2 for that reason.
- Cultivar is not a feature. Batavia Othilie (training) differs from Baby Cos and Green Oak (development) by 63% in the external study, which no recalibration can represent.
- The light response is monotonic; the development data are not.
- Root-zone temperature is required by the model and is not reported by either external study, so it is assumed equal to air temperature in both checks.
- Earth only, within the declared domains, and not validated for harvest forecasting.

## `mizuna-microbial-relative-load-v2`

*Within-campaign relative culturable aerobic microbial load on edible leaf tissue*

**Purpose.** Predict the microbial load of mizuna leaf tissue relative to its own campaign mean, in within-campaign standard deviations of log10 CFU per gram.

**Intended use.** Comparing flight and ground harvests of the same experiment on a relative scale, where the question is whether a treatment raised or lowered load rather than what the absolute load was.

**Not intended for**

- Absolute microbial load. The absolute model is retained but withdrawn from service because it does not transfer to an unseen campaign (R2 -0.55 and -0.58).
- Safety or consumability decisions. The endpoint is culturable aerobic count on leaf tissue, not pathogen presence.
- Crops other than mizuna, or campaigns other than VEG-04.

**Training data.** committed-file — `9dfffb0eddc011cd593ce11b63f5dfeeb8594e8fc8d9466df9e61eed207509c0`

  SHA-256 of data/processed/mizuna_microbial_load.csv exactly as committed. Covers 123 leaf-tissue measurements across 35 biological groups; the table is produced from the committed OSD-780 records by scripts/preprocess_osd780.py.

**Code.** `ml/training/train_microbial_v2.py` at `91884518b97bd09ee1326066679953f080ee1053` (present in commit: yes; unchanged since: yes)

**Split.** Leave-one-campaign-out transfer, with group-level cross-conformal calibration. Grouped by biological group (plant/pillow) (35 groups).

**Uncertainty.** Group-level cross-conformal interval, radius 1.679 in within-campaign standard deviations, nominal 90 % coverage, achieved 0.9919 over 35 calibration groups. The exchangeability unit is the biological group.

**Evidence.** `passes-independent-check`, decision-grade: yes. Not promoted, retrained or relabelled by this metadata completion.

**Completeness.** 15/15 checks.

**Limitations**

- Predicts RELATIVE load within a campaign. It cannot predict an absolute level for a new campaign without that campaign's own baseline samples.
- Plate counts capture only culturable aerobic organisms; total microbial load is higher and unmeasured.
- The target is microbial load on leaf tissue, not biomass, yield, growth rate or shelf life.
- logFreshWeightG is the mass of the assay subsample, not harvest biomass.
- Two campaigns only, both VEG-04 mizuna on ISS. No lettuce, tomato or radish transfer.
- Flight/ground differences may reflect cabin environment, payload handling or sampling protocol rather than gravity. Causality is not established.
- Not a food-safety clearance tool. Do not use for consumption decisions.

## `radiation-exposure-osd46-v1`

*Binary irradiated-versus-control classification from seedling expression*

**Purpose.** Classify Arabidopsis OSD-46 seedling expression into a radiation-exposure context, distinguishing gamma exposure from the design's other conditions.

**Intended use.** Reading a transcriptomic response to radiation exposure as a class, to compare against the platform's radiation model assumptions.

**Not intended for**

- Dose prediction. The model classifies context; the platform's dose capability was withdrawn in Phase 30 and this classifier does not restore it.
- Any crop other than Arabidopsis, and any platform other than the ATH1 array.
- Operational dosimetry of any kind.

**Training data.** recorded-checksum — `f15c4179dbbebdfd39c53ffb906f6251ed1fc3fa3c926efc3c71c4736eefda27`

  SHA-256 of the NASA OSDR OSD-46 v6 normalised expression matrix: 22,810 probesets x 44 samples, 23,794,742 bytes. The file is deliberately not committed (it is a 24 MB re-acquirable download, excluded by .gitignore policy); its digest is committed in data/metadata/phase9_nasa_datasets.json and the artifact recorded the same digest at training time.

**Code.** `ml/training/train_radiation_osd46.py` at `1f3c94370de4a88d1040cfa21921a59180064c86` (present in commit: yes; unchanged since: yes)

**Split.** Leave-one-biological-group-out cross-validation over 22 groups. Grouped by biological group (22 groups).

**Uncertainty.** Served as a class probability with the calibration recorded in the artifact's probabilityCalibration block; the platform reports the measured expected calibration error rather than asserting the probabilities are calibrated.

**Evidence.** `no-independent-check`, decision-grade: no. Not promoted, retrained or relabelled by this metadata completion.

**Completeness.** 15/15 checks.

**Limitations**

- Ground-based irradiation of Arabidopsis seedlings, not spaceflight and not a crop.
- Non-irradiated controls appear on only two of the three array batches and never on the batch holding 20 of the 44 arrays, so condition is associated with array batch (Cramer's V 0.506 on the full set).
- The batch-controlled evaluation on batch 0806A-10 alone is reported in supplementaryEvaluation and should be preferred over the full-set figure.
- Mixes two radiation qualities and two absorbed doses into one label; it does not separate quality from dose.
- Never predicts yield, biomass, survival or crop productivity; no such measurement exists in this study.

## `radiation-quality-osd46-v1`

*Three-class radiation quality classification from seedling expression*

**Purpose.** Classify Arabidopsis OSD-46 seedling expression into a radiation-quality class: control, gamma or high-energy heavy ion.

**Intended use.** Testing whether the platform's assumption that light and radiation quality act through distinguishable transcriptomic routes is visible in measured expression.

**Not intended for**

- Predicting plant yield or growth from radiation quality; the endpoint is a molecular class, not a phenotype.
- Crops other than Arabidopsis, or platforms other than the ATH1 array.

**Code.** `ml/training/train_radiation_osd46.py` at `1f3c94370de4a88d1040cfa21921a59180064c86` (present in commit: yes; unchanged since: yes)

**Split.** Leave-one-biological-group-out cross-validation over 22 groups. Grouped by biological group (22 groups).

**Uncertainty.** Served as a class probability with the measured calibration recorded in the artifact's probabilityCalibration block.

**Evidence.** `no-independent-check`, decision-grade: no. Not promoted, retrained or relabelled by this metadata completion.

## `tomato-flight-response-v1`

*Flight-versus-ground molecular context classification*

**Purpose.** Classify tomato seedling expression into recorded flight versus ground experimental context.

**Intended use.** Asking whether the transcriptomic record of a flight experiment is separable from its ground control at all - a question about the experiment, not about the plant's health.

**Not intended for**

- Stress severity or plant health inference. The label is recorded experimental context.
- Predicting yield, harvest mass or anything agronomic.
- Crops other than tomato, studies other than OSD-767.

**Training data.** reconstructed-derived — `88b335847f4a7d342c8c948c5369c8de990c3870f95b455ac2cd7418b2aa56b1`

  SHA-256 of the derived log1p(CPM) matrix the trainer reads: 36 samples x 33,562 genes, 29,705,842 bytes, produced by scripts/preprocess_datasets.py from the OSD-767 STAR counts. The parquet is not committed, so the digest was obtained by reconstruction from the checked upstream file, and a second, version-independent digest over the same matrix serialised as CSV is recorded alongside it because parquet byte layout is a property of the pyarrow version rather than of the data.

**Code.** `ml/training/train_models.py` at `3d79cf2e38b7b2228b9ffb227653748811d30bb7` (present in commit: yes; unchanged since: yes)

**Split.** Nested stratified group cross-validation (4 outer, 3 inner folds). Grouped by plant (both tissues of a plant stay together) (21 groups).

**Uncertainty.** Served as a class probability with the measured calibration recorded in the artifact's probabilityCalibration block. The artifact itself labels the output uncalibrated in its unit string, and the platform surfaces the measured error.

**Evidence.** `no-independent-check`, decision-grade: no. Not promoted, retrained or relabelled by this metadata completion.

**Completeness.** 15/15 checks.

**Limitations**

- One VEG-05 experiment; cannot establish cross-study or causal gravity effects.
- Flight class is not disease, health, stress severity or biomass.
- Possible flight/ground batch confounding remains despite plant-grouped validation.
- Class probability is uncalibrated, not confidence or a medical/operational diagnostic.
- Requires laboratory RNA-seq, exact gene IDs and library normalization; environmental sliders are not compatible inputs.
