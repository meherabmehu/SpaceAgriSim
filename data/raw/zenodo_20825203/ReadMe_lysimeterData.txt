DATA & FILE OVERVIEW

File List:

DATA_Nleaching.csv - daily data on seepage water in mm, nitrate concentrations in seepage water in mg/l, nitrate loads leached in kgN/ha for lysimeters included in the soil management experiment

DATA_eta.csv - daily actual evapotranspiration for weighable lysimeters included in the soil management experiment

DATA_FDR.zip - daily soil moisture measured in weighable lysimeters in four depth: 10cm, 30cm, 60cm, 90cm (with two sensor replicated by depth)

DATA_temp.csv - daily soil temperature measured in weighable lysimeters in four depth: 10cm, 30cm, 60cm, 90cm (with two sensor replicated by depth)

DATA_Tens.zip - daily soil water tension measured in weighable lysimeters in four depth: 10cm, 30cm, 60cm, 90cm (with two sensor replicated by depth)

KEY_treatmentsByLysi.csv - key to relate lysimeter IDs to treatments in the experiment (CT = conventional tillage, NIT = non-inversion tillage) and soil (G = Cambisol from Grafenried, R = Cambisol from Reckenholz, S = Luvisol from Schafisheim)
